## Academic Profile

I am a computer engineer and independent researcher whose work is situated at the intersection of machine learning, network security, and human behavior. I approach cybersecurity as a discipline that requires not only effective defenses but also interpretable, verifiable, and reproducible reasoning about why those defenses work.

## Education

- **B.Sc. in Computer Engineering**, Sakarya University, Turkey (2025)
- **B.A. in Foreign Trade** (open education, ongoing), Anadolu University, Turkey

## Research Interests

- **Explainable AI for Network Intrusion Detection:** Making the decisions of learning-based detection systems transparent, interpretable, and open to scrutiny.
- **Container and Cloud-Native Security:** Understanding attack surfaces and trust assumptions in distributed, ephemeral infrastructures.
- **Human-Centered Cybersecurity:** Examining how user behavior and cognitive factors shape the success of phishing and other social attacks.

## Methodological and Technical Foundation

My research is supported by a practical engineering background in full-stack software development, database systems, and system architecture. This background allows me to move between theoretical questions and concrete implementations, treating secure design and empirical validation as complementary parts of the same inquiry.

## Research Philosophy

I regard scientific work as valuable to the extent that it can be tested, criticized, and reproduced. My aim is to contribute to the cybersecurity community through open, rigorous, and practice-oriented research.
