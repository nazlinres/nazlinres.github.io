I am **Nazlı Nur Esmeray**, a computer engineer and independent researcher based in Kocaeli, Turkey. I was born in 2001 and graduated from the Department of Computer Engineering at Sakarya University in March 2025.

My research interests are shaped around the theoretical and methodological foundations of cybersecurity. My work proceeds along three axes:

- **Explainable AI for network intrusion detection systems (XAI for NIDS):** The question of how, why, and to what extent the decisions of machine learning-based detection models can be made interpretable
- **Container and cloud-native security:** An examination of the attack surface and security assumptions in distributed and dynamic infrastructures
- **Human-centered cybersecurity:** The role of user behavior and cognitive factors in security, in the context of phishing attacks

While continuing my cybersecurity research, I am currently pursuing an open education degree in Foreign Trade at Anadolu University (AÖF).

My engineering background rests on full-stack web development, database management, and system architecture. I work with C#, .NET Core, SQL, Node.js, Angular, React, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, MongoDB, and Docker. I aim to use this experience to approach complex problems systematically, to ground application security in sound principles, and to contribute to original research questions.

I believe that scientific knowledge is valuable only to the extent that it can be tested, criticized, and reproduced. Accordingly, I aim to contribute to the cybersecurity literature through reproducible, open, and practice-oriented work.
